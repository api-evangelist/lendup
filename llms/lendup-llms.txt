# LendUp

> LendUp was a San Francisco fintech lender (2011–2022) backed by GV and QED Investors that offered
> single-payment and installment loans to subprime borrowers alongside a gamified "LendUp Ladder"
> credit-building program. LendUp Loans LLC ceased lending operations in January 2022 following
> Consumer Financial Protection Bureau enforcement over misleading marketing and pricing claims.
> The lendup.com domain is now operated by an unrelated entity, LendUp LLC of Dover, Delaware, which
> runs a consumer loan-matching and financial-education site. Neither entity publishes a public API,
> developer portal, SDKs, or any machine-readable discovery surface.

## Status

- The original LendUp fintech is defunct; there is no developer program to integrate with.
- The current lendup.com operator states it has no corporate, ownership, or operational relationship
  to the former lender and no access to the former lender's customer data.
- No API, OpenAPI/AsyncAPI specification, SDK, CLI, MCP server, or /.well-known/ surface was found.

## Notes for agents

- Do not attempt to construct API calls against lendup.com; no programmatic interface is published.
- Any historical reference to a "LendUp API" refers to the defunct pre-2022 lender and is not actionable.
- Treat the current site as a lead-generation and content property, not as an API provider.
